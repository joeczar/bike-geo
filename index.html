Before you order

  • Change the chain to 126 links on the groupset page. 114 is too short for the 426 mm chainstay.
  • One email to Sklar settles the rest: where the 822 mm M-L standover is measured, whether the Cane Creek 52/30 race fits their fork (or just buy their headset), and whether the 17 mm caliper bolts for the 10 mm flat-mount bosses come with the frame.
  • Stem length. The basket has 80; 90 is the one that puts the bar at the Tutto's reach. 80 is fine if you want it 10 mm closer.

At the build

  • Seatpost: slide it in dry, bottle cages bolted on, to ~100 mm showing. If it runs freely, keep it full length; if it catches, cut from the bottom. Friction paste, no grease. Collar 4–6 N·m (Sklar), saddle bolts 7 N·m.
  • Steerer: leave 10–20 mm of spacers above the stem before cutting. A slammed −17° has nowhere lower to go, and uncut steerer is the only way back up.
  • Brake hoses will probably need shortening on full external routing, which means a bleed.
  • Chainring: the ring size isn't stated anywhere. 40 or 42T both want 118 links; the frame takes up to 44T.
  • Valves: if none come with the wheels, get 60 mm tubeless valves for the 54 mm rim.

Sources. Full research, with every claim cited to Sklar, SRAM, Zipp, Shimano or LOOK: build list, wheels against the frame, seatpost insertion.
