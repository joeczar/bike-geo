# Zipp 303 XPLR S on a Sklar SuperSomething V2

Researched 18 Oct 2026. All sources accessed 18 Oct 2026. Primary sources only (zipp.com support, sram.com, SRAM PDFs, Zipp's embedded tyre chart, sklarbikes.com); no reviews or retailers used.

## Verdict

1. **Fits mechanically**: 12x100 / 12x142 thru-axle end caps, centre-lock rotors, 700c. Nothing on the frame blocks it.
2. **Tyre size is the real constraint**: Zipp says a labelled tyre grows ~3-4 mm on this 32 mm rim. A labelled 45 mounts at ~49 mm, inside Sklar's 52. A labelled 50 mounts at ~53, over it.
3. **Approved tyres only**: labelled 40-57 mm tyres must be on Zipp's list. Goodyear Fitment XPLR tyres are already labelled at their mounted width, so their 50 really is 50.
4. **Drivers are XDR or SRAM/Shimano road-splined only**, with no Microspline (that's the SW). SRAM 13-speed XPLR and Transmission XD cassettes (with a 1.85 mm spacer) are fine. Shimano MTB 12-speed is not.
5. **Bikepacking needs care**: for loaded use Zipp says to pick tyres of 58 mm or more, which this frame cannot clear. As a fast-gravel wheel it suits the frame; as a loaded bikepacking wheel on this frame it is off Zipp's own advice.

## Naming: 303 XPLR S vs 303 XPLR SW

- **303 XPLR S**, model codes `WH-303-XPS-A1` and `WH-303-XPS-B1`. 76/176 DB hubs, CX-Sprint spokes, 1610-1642 g. This is the wheel described here.
  - Source: https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/frame-fit-specifications/zipp/zipp-wheel-and-hub-specifications.pdf (Zipp Wheel and Hub Specifications, GEN.0000000006258 Rev N, pp. 28-29)
- **303 XPLR SW**, `WH-303-XPSW-A1`. Same 32/40 mm rim, but with a ZR1 hub ("66 points of engagement"), CX-Ray spokes, 1496 g, and Microspline/N3W driver options.
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27051237923739-What-hub-is-used-on-the-303-XPLR-SW-wheelset
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27051275728283-How-much-do-the-303-XPLR-SW-wheels-weigh
  - same specs PDF, p. 28
- **303 S** (`WH-303-STLD-A1`, 23 mm internal) is a different, narrower wheel. Don't confuse it with the XPLR S.
  - Zipp tyre chart: https://www.sram.com/en/zipp/campaigns/hookless-tire-compatibility
- **A1 vs B1**: both are sold as "303 XPLR S" and share one spare-parts page. The differences the spec sheet lists:
  - nipples: A1 brass, B1 aluminium
  - rear drive-side spoke: A1 256 mm, B1 254 mm
  - system weight limit: A1 297 lb, B1 298 lb (both 135 kg)
  - B1 also comes in a white decal option
  - Sources: specs PDF pp. 28-29; 2026 Zipp Spare Parts Catalog p. 17, https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/spare-parts/spare-parts-catalog/2026-zipp-spare-parts-catalog.pdf

## Tyres: approved list, widths, pressure

- **Where the approved list lives**: https://www.sram.com/en/zipp/campaigns/hookless-tire-compatibility
  - The list is an embedded Softr app, so it only appears with JavaScript: https://humberto53306.softr.app/embed/pages/8b14e130-6740-4c4e-aa63-7cf3cf924803/blocks/list1
  - Read in a browser, entry "303 XPLR S, WH-303-XPS-A1: Tire width range 40 - 60mm, Internal width 32mm".
- **Zipp's rule** (chart compatibility notes):
  - Rim follows ISO 5775-2 for a 700x32 TSS rim.
  - ISO 5775-1 recommends 58-83 mm labels on that rim.
  - Zipp approves selected models labelled **40-57 mm**, list only.
  - Any ISO TSS tyre labelled 58-60 mm is approved. **60 mm is the max.**
  - Also: https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28079301489691-With-303-XPLR-S-wheels-can-I-use-the-maximum-tire-size-allowed-by-my-bike-frame
- **Min 40 mm / max 60 mm**: chart above; specs PDF p. 28 ("Tubeless Minimum: 40 mm").
- **Examples on the list that would clear this frame (≤45 labelled)**:
  - Goodyear Fitment XPLR Slick 40/45, XPLR Inter 45/50, Vector R XPLR 40
  - Hutchinson Caracal / Touareg / Overide 40/45
  - Maxxis Rambler/Ravager/Reaver 40/45
  - Panaracer Gravelking X1/X1+/X1R 45
  - Rene Herse Snoqualmie / Corkscrew / Manastash 44 Endurance
  - Schwalbe G-One R / RS / Allround / Overland / Bite / Ultrabite 40/45
  - Teravail Rutland 42, Washburn 42, Updraft 40/45
  - Tufo Speedero / Swampero / Thundero 40/44
  - Ultradynamico Cava 42, Rosé 45
  - Vittoria Terreno Pro 45, Corsa Pro Control 42
  - WTB Raddler 44, Resolute 42, Riddler 45, Vulpine 45
  - Zipp Tangente G40
  - The full list is in the chart. It changes, so check it at purchase.
- **Tubes are allowed**, but still only with approved tyres.
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27049941035803-Can-I-run-my-303-XPLR-S-wheel-with-tubes-and-tires-instead-of-tubeless
- **Max pressure by labelled width**:
  - 40-44 mm: 3.5 bar / 51 psi
  - 45-54 mm: 3.0 bar / 44 psi
  - 55-64 mm: 2.5 bar / 36 psi

  - The general TSS ceiling is 5 bar / 72 psi (hookless compatibility page).

## Mounted width vs label, against the 52 mm clearance

- **Zipp FAQ**: 40-46 mm tyres "grow ~3.6mm"; 47-60 mm "grow ~2.8mm".
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28079301489691-With-303-XPLR-S-wheels-can-I-use-the-maximum-tire-size-allowed-by-my-bike-frame
- **Zipp chart notes**: add 4 mm (40-46) or 3 mm (47-60). Goodyear Fitment XPLR tyres are labelled at their installed width on the 303 XPLR. "Ensure it allows 4mm of clearance with the frame."
- **Clearance FAQ**: "account for an additional 3-4mm of tire width".
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27050180504987-How-much-frame-clearance-is-required-for-the-303-S-XPLR-wheels
- **Standards background**: ISO/ETRTO harmonised in 2023 so that a tyre may not deviate more than 2 mm from its marked size. That is why Zipp gates tyre sizes by rim width.
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27459000627611-Why-did-the-rim-and-tire-recommendations-change-for-hookless-wheels

Estimates (my arithmetic from Zipp's figures; not measured):

| Labelled tyre | Zipp growth | Est. mounted | vs Sklar 52 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45 (non-Goodyear) | +3.6 to +4 | ~48.6-49 | inside, ~3 mm spare |
| 47-48 | +2.8 to +3 | ~50-51 | marginal |
| 50 (non-Goodyear) | +2.8 to +3 | ~52.8-53 | over |
| Goodyear XPLR Inter 50 | label = installed | ~50 | inside, ~2 mm spare |

**Unverified**:
- Sklar does not say which rim width its 52 mm is measured on, or whether it already includes a mud margin.
- Zipp asks for 4 mm of frame clearance on top of mounted width. Read strictly against a 52 mm rating, that would cap the mounted width at 48 (i.e. a labelled ~44-45).
- Sklar separately advises 48 mm max in mud.
- Measure the actual tyre on the rim before relying on a 47+.

**Diameter**: the rider's current tyres are ~710 mm OD. A labelled 45 on this rim should land around 712-716 mm OD (622 + 2 × ~45-47 mm height). **Unverified**: this is a rule-of-thumb estimate, and no primary source gives tyre height on this rim.

## Drivetrain: UDH / Transmission on 142

- **The wheel imposes nothing on UDH.** The derailleur interface belongs to the frame. Sklar states Full Mount Transmission compatibility (frame page above).
- **SRAM supports 142 mm OLD with Transmission**:
  - "The 52 mm chainline must be used with 142 mm OLD MTB frames."
  - "For 142 mm OLD ROAD frames, a 52 mm chainline is optimal, but a 47.5 mm chainline DUB ROAD crank can be used."
  - Min chainstay for 142 / 47.5 gravel: 415 mm.
  - Source: Eagle Transmission frame-fit spec, GEN.0000000008642 Rev E, https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/frame-fit-specifications/mtb/eagle-transmission-and-dh-transmission-frame-fit-specifications.pdf
- **SRAM 13-speed (XPLR) on 142**: Wide/XPLR 1x needs 47.5 chainline and a 415 mm min chainstay, and "Full Mount Rear Derailleur is only compatible with the UDH Frame interface".
  - 2024 Road frame-fit, GEN.0000000007844 Rev J: https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/frame-fit-specifications/road/2024-road-frame-fit-specifications.pdf
  - **Unverified**: the SuperSomething's chainstay length was not checked here against 415.
- **Wheel side**:
  - 13-speed XPLR cassettes need XDR: available.
  - Eagle / Transmission XD cassettes go on XDR with a 1.85 mm spacer.
  - Shimano GRX 12-speed uses the road-splined body: available. **Unverified**: no Zipp doc names GRX specifically.
  - Shimano MTB 12-speed needs Microspline: **not available** on the S.

## Other mismatch risks

- **Bikepacking**: Zipp's own guidance for bikepacking is "a tire that is on the Tire Compatibility Guide and is 58mm/2.28" or wider".
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27050469003291-Can-the-303-XPLR-S-wheels-be-used-for-road-or-bikepacking-applications
  - The SuperSomething clears 52. So for loaded touring on this frame you'd be running narrower tyres than Zipp recommends. It is not prohibited, but it is against their advice.
  - Rider + bike + bags must stay under 135 kg.
- **Tyre choice is narrowed** to the approved list for anything labelled 40-57 mm.
- **XC MTB is outside intended use for the SW**, which voids its warranty. The S FAQ only says "fastest on gravel".
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27050656237851-Can-the-303-XPLR-SW-wheel-be-used-for-XC-MTB
  - https://support.zipp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27050679467035-What-is-the-intended-riding-application-of-the-303-XPLR-S-wheelset
- **Fender line**: Sklar quotes fenders with 700x50 tyres. A ~49 mm mounted tyre should fit, but check the fender model.
