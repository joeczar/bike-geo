## 1. What the frameset ships with (Sklar)

Source: https://www.sklarbikes.com/supersomething (spec table + FAQ).

- **Included**: "All frames ship with a seat post clamp and thru axles included as well as cable guide clips."
  - Seat collar: "27.2mm, 30mm seat collar included."
  - Thru-axles: rear 164 mm, 12 x 1.5; front 120 mm, 12 x 1.5.
  - Cable guide clips. Full external routing, so no other routing hardware is needed.
- **Not included**:
  - **Headset**: the page says "Don't forget a matching headset".
  - **Spacers, top cap and star nut**: not mentioned for the frameset. "Headset/Spacers - Sklar" appears only in the complete-build kits.
- **Headset standard** (FAQ): "make sure your headset is EC34/28.6 upper, EC44/30 lower." Steel fork steerer is 1-1/8" straight. Fork weight 1100 g uncut.
- **UDH hanger**: not listed as included. Sklar sells one as a replacement small part (8 USD, https://www.sklarbikes.com/products/small-parts). Irrelevant here, because the Rival XPLR AXS derailleur is **Full Mount** and bolts to the UDH interface without a hanger (bike24 groupset description: "Full Mount-Interface"). Sklar: "UDH Dropout compatible with all rear derailleurs including SRAM Transmission full mount".
  - **Unverified**: that the supplied 12 x 1.5 axle is the one Sklar intends for Full Mount use. Ask Sklar when ordering.
- **Brakes**: "Flat mount (160mm direct), bosses are 10mm thick."
- **Chainring**: "Up to 44T single chainring."
- **Chainstay**: 426 mm on every size.

## 3. Rotor size

- **Sklar**: "Flat mount (160mm direct)". No maximum or minimum is listed anywhere on the page or FAQ.
- **SRAM**: the caliper mounting spec (GEN.0000000005232 Rev V) mounts a flat-mount caliper on a 160-direct mount with no bracket. A 180 rotor needs a +20 bracket (F20/FD20), and SRAM's FAQ says 180 on flat mount needs a frame built for it.
  - https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/frame-fit-specifications/aftermarket/disc-brake-caliper-mounting-specifications-for-road-and-mtb.pdf
  - https://support.sram.com/hc/en-us/articles/6068363789723-What-size-rotors-can-I-use-with-Rival-eTap-AXS
- **Recommendation: 160 / 160.** Going to 180 on Sklar's steel fork is not supported by anything Sklar publishes; ask them first.
- **Rotor**: SRAM Paceline A2 centrelock (RT-PLN-A2), 140/160. bike24: "Centerlock-Version mit Innenverzahnung. Inklusive SRAM-Lockring." 160 mm is 37,99 €. The Zipp wheels also include lockrings (wheel note), so you'll have spares.
- **Bolts**: for 10 mm bosses, SRAM's chart calls for **17 mm button-head bolts**, direct mount, both ends (caliper PDF p. 9).
  - **Unverified**: which bolt lengths come in the E1 caliper box. Check before assembly. If they're missing, it's a few-euro part, not a blocker.

## 4. Bottom bracket

- **The crank is DUB Wide.** SRAM: wide cranks need a BB labelled "WIDE" (e.g. "BB DUB BSA 68 ROAD WIDE") because the spindle is 5 mm longer. Standard spacers won't work.
  - https://support.sram.com/hc/en-us/articles/6085834686235-Are-Rival-43-30T-and-1x-wide-cranksets-compatible-with-the-same-bottom-brackets-as-SRAM-s-other-DUB-road-cranksets
- **SRAM DUB Road chart** (GEN0000000005976 Rev G):
  - DUB BSA 68 Road-Wide is part `00.6418.015.004`.
  - Spacers for BSA 68 Road-Wide: **2.5 mm outer left, 5.5 mm outer right**.
  - https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/compatibility-map/road/dub-choose-a-road-bottom-bracket-compatibility-map.pdf
- **Don't buy the "DUB MTB Wide" BB** (bike24 p1474712). That is the 55 mm MTB chainline, with 3/2.5/2.5/7.5 spacers (DUB MTB chart).
  - https://www.sram.com/globalassets/document-hierarchy/compatibility-map/mtb/dub-mtb-bottom-bracket-compatibility-chart.pdf
- **bike24 options**:
  - **Preferred**: SRAM DUB Road Lagerschalen BSA-68-DUB-Wide (SRA480221), 32,99 €. "Passend ausschließlich für DUB Wide Kurbeln." **Currently "nicht lieferbar".**
  - **In stock**: SRAM DUB BSA-68/73 cups (p1264013, 30,89 €) plus spacers. SRAM's own Road-Wide spacer kit 11.6418.002.002 (p1464197) is also not deliverable. Hope's "Spacer Kit für DUB - ROAD WIDE | 47.5mm Kettenlinie" (p1917171, 7,99 €) is in stock.
    - **Unverified**: that the Hope kit contains exactly 2.5 + 5.5 mm. Check the listing before relying on it.

## 8. Incompatibilities and flags in the current basket

1. **Chain: 114 links is too short.** SRAM's Full Mount Chain Length Guide (axs.sram.com/guides/fullmount/chain/calculator), drop bar / XPLR (Full Mount) / 426 mm / 40T, returns **"118 Links"**, "Supports Chainrings 40T & 42T", setup cog 21T (6).
   - Docs: https://docs.sram.com/en-US/publications/6sfLCOGTn6FE98W8vXLqm0
   - **Fix**: switch the groupset option to 126 links (+2 €) and cut to 118.
   - **Unverified**: the 44T figure.
2. **BB must be Road-Wide**, not standard DUB and not MTB Wide (§4). The groupset doesn't include one.
3. **Stem / steerer / bar**: Zipp SC SL stem is 31.8 bar clamp on a 28.6 (1-1/8") steerer clamp, which matches the straight steel steerer and the 31.8 SC SL 70 XPLR bar. No conflict.
   - The **fork is uncut**: measure stack and cut once the spacer height is decided.
   - Steel steerer, so use a star nut.
4. **Stem angle (fit note, not a fault)**: the basket's stem is **-17° / 80 mm**. The project's `sklar.html` dial-in panel B opens at Joe's current **+6°** setup (35 mm spacers / 80 / +6). A -17° stem puts the bar markedly lower than that panel shows, unless the spacer stack goes up to compensate. Check it in `sklar.html` before cutting the steerer.
5. **Brake hose length: unverified.** SRAM doesn't publish E1 hose lengths in the pages checked. With full external routing on an M-L, expect to shorten the rear (and maybe the front) hose. SRAM says shortening requires a bleed (HRD Hose Shortening and Bleed Manual, linked from https://www.sram.com/en/service/models/ed-riv-e1).
   - Either have the shop do it, or add a SRAM DOT 5.1 bleed kit. bike24 lists "SRAM Bremsen-Entlüftungsset V2 Pro - DOT 5.1 - 00.5318.045.002" at 70,99 €; product link not captured.
6. **Caliper bolts**: the 10 mm bosses need 17 mm button-head bolts (§3). Contents of the E1 caliper box are unverified.
7. **Chainline / chainstay**: 47.5 mm Wide crank on 142 OLD with a 426 mm chainstay is within SRAM's 13-speed XPLR rule (min 415 mm, 47.5 chainline). Settled; see the wheel note.
8. **Seatpost**: the 27.2 post matches; the 30 mm collar is included. Insertion length is being researched separately and is not covered here.
9. **Tyre width**: a non-Goodyear 50 would mount at ~53 mm, which is over Sklar's 52 (wheel note). Stay at labelled 45, or Zipp/Goodyear XPLR 50.
